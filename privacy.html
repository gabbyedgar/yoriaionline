Privacy policy

YORIAI Platform — yoriai.online

Effective: August 23, 2026 Last updated: August 23, 2026 Contact: info@yoriai.online

At YORIAI, your privacy is fundamental to how we operate. This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, how we use and protect it, and your rights as a user.

1. Who we are

YORIAI (yoriai.online) is an experience-based social platform connecting locals and travelers in Japan. We operate as the data controller for personal information collected through our platform. If you have questions about this policy, contact us at info@yoriai.online.

2. What data we collect

2.1 Account & identity data

Collected when you register:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Password (stored in encrypted/hashed form, never in plain text)
  • Profile photo (optional)
  • Nationality or country of origin (optional)
  • Language preferences

2.2 Profile & interest data

Collected to personalize your experience:

  • Biography / personal introduction
  • Interests and activity preferences (e.g., food, culture, outdoor activities)
  • Travel status (local resident or traveler)
  • Languages spoken

2.3 Location data

Collected to match users with nearby experiences:

  • City or region (required for functionality)
  • Approximate GPS location, if you grant permission via your device
  • We do not continuously track location; access is request-based only

2.4 Booking & transaction data

Collected when you make or receive bookings:

  • Experience bookings (date, activity, host details)

2.5 Communications data

Collected through in-platform messaging:

  • Messages exchanged between travelers and hosts on the platform
  • Notification preferences
  • Support tickets and correspondence with the YORIAI team

2.6 Reviews & ratings data

Collected after experiences:

  • Ratings given and received
  • Written reviews submitted about hosts or travelers

2.7 Usage & device data

Collected automatically when you use the platform:

  • Device type and operating system
  • Browser type and version
  • IP address
  • Pages viewed, features used, and time spent on the platform
  • Referral source (how you arrived at yoriai.online)
  • Cookies and similar tracking technologies (see Section 7)

2.8 Host-specific data

Collected from users who register as hosts:

  • Government-issued ID or identity verification document (for vetting, stored securely and not shared publicly)
  • Experience listings (title, description, photos, capacity, availability)
  • Host performance metrics (response rate, completion rate, ratings)

4. How we share your data

YORIAI does not sell your personal data. We share data only in the following circumstances:

4.1 With other users (limited)

When you book an experience, your name and profile photo are shared with the host. When you list an experience, your name, photo, and ratings are visible to platform users.

4.2 With service providers

We work with trusted third-party processors including:

  • Cloud hosting and infrastructure providers
  • Analytics providers (anonymized/aggregated data only)
  • Email communication services

All third-party processors are contractually bound to protect your data.

4.3 Legal requirements

We may disclose data if required by law, court order, or to protect the safety of our users or the public.

4.4 Business transfers

In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, user data may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will notify you before such a transfer and provide the option to delete your account.

5. Data retention

  • Active account data is retained for as long as your account is active
  • Transaction records are retained for 7 years to comply with tax and financial regulations
  • ID verification documents for hosts are deleted within 12 months of the account closure
  • You may request deletion of your account and data at any time (see Section 6)

7. Cookies

YORIAI uses cookies and similar technologies to operate the platform and improve user experience:

  • Essential cookies: Required for core platform functionality (login sessions, security)
  • Analytics cookies: Help us understand how users interact with the platform
  • Preference cookies: Remember your settings and preferences
  • Marketing cookies: Used only with your explicit consent to personalize promotions

You can manage cookie preferences in your browser settings. Disabling essential cookies may affect platform functionality.

8. Data security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data:

  • All data transmitted is encrypted via HTTPS/TLS
  • Passwords are hashed using secure algorithms
  • Access to user data within YORIAI is role-based and limited to authorized personnel
  • We conduct regular security reviews and vulnerability assessments

Despite our efforts, no system is 100% secure. In the event of a data breach, we will notify affected users in accordance with applicable law.

9. Children's privacy

YORIAI is not intended for users under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors. If we become aware that a user is under 18, we will delete their account and associated data promptly.

10. International transfers

YORIAI primarily processes data within Japan. If data is transferred to other countries for hosting or processing purposes, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place, including contractual data protection clauses.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. We will notify users of material changes via email or a platform notification. The "Last Updated" date at the top of this document reflects the most recent revision.

12. Contact & complaints

For privacy questions, requests, or complaints:

  • Email: info@yoriai.online
  • Website: yoriai.online

If you believe we have not handled your data appropriately, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the relevant data protection authority in your jurisdiction.
