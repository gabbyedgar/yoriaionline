Safety & trust

Strangers, yes.
Unvetted, never.

Yoriai facilitates real meetings between people who haven't met. We treat that responsibility as the most important thing we do — built in from day one, not bolted on after something goes wrong.

信
Before you ever meet

Trust signals you can actually see.

Complete profiles only

A photo, a real bio (200+ characters) and a verified email are required before anyone can join or host. Incomplete profiles are blocked — no exceptions.

Visible review history

Every profile shows a star rating and a count of completed activities. New members carry a "New" badge so you always know who's who.

Host approval

Hosts review a traveler's full profile before approving. It's a mutual vetting system — both sides choose to meet.

Full transparency

Every listing shows the exact time, the public location, the participant list and the host's reviews. No surprises.

If something feels off

Mechanisms, not just promises.

⚑
Report system

Predefined categories — harassment, scam, no-show, safety concern. Reports go straight to a moderation queue, resolved in under 24 hours (under 12 by month six).

⊘
One-tap block

Block anyone instantly. Blocked members become completely invisible to each other across the entire platform.

◎
Public spaces only

Community guidelines require public meeting points. Any listing specifying a private address is automatically flagged for review.

◍
Small groups by design

Activities cap at 2–10 people. Small groups are inherently safer — and they build trust faster, too.
