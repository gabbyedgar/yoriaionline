Why Yoriai, why now

Travel changed. The world wants smaller, realer, warmer — and nobody serves the everyday, peer-to-peer space.

Airbnb Experiences wants professional hosts. GetYourGuide and Viator sell tour-company tickets. We think the most meaningful thing about a place isn't a product at all — it's the person who lives there, doing what they already do, willing to let you come along.

門

The threshold

The torii is three strokes — two pillars, one crossbar. The everyday on one side, something sacred on the other. Yoriai lives in the doorway between them.

人

The human

One terracotta dot beneath the gate. The traveler, the local — whoever arrives first. The smallest possible mark carrying the entire promise: a person, standing at the threshold.

縁

The connection

Japanese has a word — en — for the invisible thread that ties people together by chance. Every activity on Yoriai is a small bet that the thread is worth pulling.

The five-year arc

From one Osaka morning to a social layer for every city.

Year 1
一

Community marketplace

Prove the model in Osaka. Locals post consistently, travelers join consistently. Product-market fit: 65%+ host retention, 4.5+ average rating.

Years 2–3
二

Experience platform

Expand across Japan. Introduce tipping, premium experiences and host analytics. Launch the Verified Host program.

Years 4–5
三

Cultural network

The world's largest network of local cultural ambassadors. Beyond tourism — language exchange, relocation, belonging. A social layer for every city.

Our operating belief

Speed beats perfection.

Ship fast. Measure what matters — completed activities per week. Iterate on what people do, not what they say. The team that moves fastest and listens hardest wins.
